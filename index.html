Signs Feeding Is Going Well

A gentle overview to help you feel reassured, not medical advice.

  • 3–4 wet nappies a day by day four, increasing from there
  • Steady weight gain once feeding is established
  • Your baby seems settled and satisfied after most feeds

About

Our Story

I created Beginning Haven because everything I needed in those first few months with my own children simply wasn't available in one place — so I built it myself.

I have created gentle, practical guides for the earliest weeks of motherhood, so you have something steady to reach for, whenever you need it. Every guide, checklist, and journal page is written the way I'd want a friend to talk to me — honestly, warmly, and without judgement.
